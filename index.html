About Nicole

Artistry without boundaries.

Nicole Hardin

Nicole Hardin is a multifaceted music professional whose work spans music production, composition, orchestration, arranging, conducting, studio recording, and live performance.

As a session and performing/touring musician, educator, and consultant, she brings artistic versatility, technical expertise, and creative leadership to projects across genres and performance settings.

Her experience encompasses contemporary music, orchestral performance, television, recording sessions, and live productions. With a deep understanding of instrumental writing and collaborative artistry, Nicole approaches each project with a commitment to musical excellence and creative expression.

An educator at heart, Nicole values musical development, mentorship, and the relationships that make meaningful collaboration possible.

Nicole served as an elected Governor of the Recording Academy Nashville Chapter from 2023–2025.

Selected Original Works & Commissions

Music imagined. Stories brought to life.

Commissioned works and orchestral projects that celebrate musical heritage, reinterpret repertoire, and bring new dimensions to contemporary performance.

2022 · Nashville Symphony Orchestra

Young Warrior — Reimagined

Commissioned by the Nashville Symphony Orchestra

Commissioned to reimagine Young Warrior, associated with James Weldon Johnson and Harry T. Burleigh, Nicole Hardin developed a full-orchestra arrangement that explores the work through a fresh harmonic and expressive perspective. Through thoughtful chord substitutions, expanded orchestral textures, and a deliberate departure from a traditionally patriotic interpretation, she created a more nuanced and contemporary musical experience while honoring the work's historical roots.

Arranger & Orchestrator · Full Orchestra

2026 · Atlanta Music Project

Cape Verdean Suite

Commissioned by Atlanta Music Project (AMP)

Commissioned by Atlanta Music Project, Cape Verdean Suite is an original orchestral work celebrating the rich musical heritage of Cape Verde. Drawing upon the islands' distinctive musical traditions, rhythms, and melodic expressions, Nicole composed and arranged the suite for full orchestra, weaving cultural influences into a vibrant symphonic narrative that honors Cape Verdean identity and musical storytelling.

Composer & Arranger · Full Orchestra

2026 · Live Recording & Video Production

Flavour: Orchestrated

Commissioned Orchestral Arrangements

Commissioned to arrange and orchestrate six selections to date for Nigerian artist Flavour, Nicole brings the energy, rhythmic complexity, and stylistic character of African popular music into a full orchestral setting. Her arrangements integrate the music's African rhythmic foundations and genre influences with expansive orchestral textures, creating a dynamic dialogue between contemporary African music and symphonic expression.

Arranger & Orchestrator · Full Orchestra
