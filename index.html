About Nicole

Artistry without boundaries.

Nicole Hardin

Nicole Hardin is a multifaceted music professional whose work spans music production, composition, orchestration, arranging, conducting, studio recording, and live performance.

As a session and performing/touring musician, educator, and consultant, she brings artistic versatility, technical expertise, and creative leadership to projects across genres and performance settings.

Her experience encompasses contemporary music, orchestral performance, television, recording sessions, and live productions. With a deep understanding of instrumental writing and collaborative artistry, Nicole approaches each project with a commitment to musical excellence and creative expression.

An educator at heart, Nicole values musical development, mentorship, and the relationships that make meaningful collaboration possible.

Nicole served as an elected Governor of the Recording Academy Nashville Chapter from 2023–2025.
