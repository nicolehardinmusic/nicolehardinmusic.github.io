Who we are

Two identities. One commitment to excellence.

The Artist

Nicole Hardin

Composer, arranger, orchestrator, conductor, and musician contractor working across contemporary music, orchestral performance, television, and live productions.

Nicole brings a deep understanding of instrumental writing, collaborative artistry, and the people who bring music from score to stage.

Nicole served as an elected Governor of the Recording Academy Nashville Chapter from 2023–2025.

The Company

Omnia Session Strings

Founded by Nicole and Hamilton Hardin, Omnia Session Strings provides orchestral and string services for recording artists, producers, and live productions.

From assembling exceptional musicians to coordinating sessions and delivering compelling performances, Omnia brings artistry and organization together.

Services include string and orchestral recording, personnel contracting, ensemble coordination, and creative production support.
